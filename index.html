WHO WE ARE

Small Actions. Big Changes.

Zachary and Sam is an initiative created to encourage people to take action against poverty.

We believe that helping others does not always require a lot of money. Giving your time, sharing resources and supporting communities can make a real difference.

Our goal is to bring people together and create a world where everyone has the opportunity to live with dignity.
